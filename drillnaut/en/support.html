FAQ

How do I play Drillnaut?

Tap the rock to dig and buy drills at the bottom. Drag two identical drills onto each other – they merge into one of the next level. The stronger your drills, the faster the big drill digs down and the more coins you earn. Spend coins on upgrades and new worlds.

My grid is full – what now?

Merge two identical drills to free a slot. Drills below your current shop level are lifted automatically, so there are always pairs. The “Workshop Expansion” upgrade gives you more slots.

Why is it suddenly so slow?

Every world gets harder the deeper you go. Below the world bottom (from depth 100) the Deep Abyss begins, where blocks are especially hard. That’s the time to buy upgrades, merge drills, travel to the next world or prestige. An hourglass next to the depth shows how long the current block will take.

What is prestige?

With prestige you restart your mine and receive relic cores. Spend them on permanent ancient technology that works in every run – so you progress much faster next time.

Does my mine keep digging when the app is closed?

Yes. When you return you collect your offline earnings – up to a time limit you can extend with the “Night Shift” upgrade. An optional video doubles them.

Where is my progress saved?

On your iPhone and – if you are signed in to iCloud – also in your iCloud account. After reinstalling or on a new iPhone, Drillnaut asks whether to load your progress from iCloud. You can turn the backup off under Settings → iCloud.

My purchase didn’t arrive.

Close Drillnaut completely and restart it – pending purchases are delivered automatically at launch. “No Ads”, the packs and “Permanent 2× Mining” can be restored under Settings → Restore purchases, for example on a new iPhone.

If that doesn’t help, write to us with the purchase date. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I turn off the ads?

Yes – with “No Ads + Instant Rewards” in the shop there are no more full-screen ads, and all video rewards are granted instantly without a video. The “Ultimate Mining Pack” includes it too.

How do I change my privacy and ad settings?

In the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices (shown where consent is required, e.g. in the EU and Switzerland). You change app tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

I can’t hear any sound.

Check the silent switch or Action button and your iPhone’s volume. In Drillnaut you can turn music, sound effects and haptics on or off under Settings.
