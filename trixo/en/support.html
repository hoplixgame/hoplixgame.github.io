Frequently asked questions

How do I play Trixo?

Drag the colorful pieces from the tray onto the outline. Every piece is made of triangles and snaps into the grid when it fits. You win when the whole outline is filled. Tap a piece on the outline to send it back, or simply drag it away.

How do I rotate a piece?

From level 12, some pieces are turned the wrong way. Tap a piece in the tray – it rotates by 60° each time.

Can every level be solved?

Yes. Every level is built from its own pieces, so there is always at least one solution – often several. Any arrangement that fills the outline completely counts.

What do hints and skipping do?

A hint puts one piece in its correct spot (for coins, or free with an optional video). From level 5 you can skip a level if you are stuck for a while. You earn coins for every solved level, for the Daily Puzzle and in the shop.

What is the Daily Puzzle?

There is a new puzzle every day – the same for all players. Solve it several days in a row to grow your streak; every 7 days you get a bonus.

My purchase didn’t arrive.

Close Trixo completely and start it again – pending purchases are delivered automatically at launch. You can get “Remove Ads”, the Starter Pack and “All Colour Themes” back under Settings → Restore purchases, e.g. on a new iPhone.

If that doesn’t help, write to us with the purchase date. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I turn off the ads?

Yes – “Remove Ads” in the shop removes full-screen ads for good and adds 300 coins. The one-time Starter Pack includes “Remove Ads” too. After any other purchase, Trixo also stops showing full-screen ads. You can still use optional reward videos if you like.

How do I change my privacy and ad settings?

In the app under Settings → Privacy options (shown where consent is required, e.g. in the EU and Switzerland). App tracking can be changed in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, your progress is lost. When you move to a new iPhone with an iCloud or computer backup, it is transferred too.

I can’t hear any sound.

Check the silent switch or Action button and your iPhone’s volume. In Trixo you can turn sound and vibration on and off under Settings.
