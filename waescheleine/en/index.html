🧺 Straight onto the line

Drag shirts, socks, jeans and sheets out of the basket and peg them to the line. Every piece pulls it a little lower.

💦 Nothing may get wet

If anything dips into the paddling pool, the roses or the grass, the level is lost. Heavy pieces belong close to the posts.

⭐ Three stars

The straighter the line hangs at the end, the more stars you earn. Matching colours side by side bring coins.

🌹 50 levels in 5 chapters

From the sunny garden through the rose bed and a windy afternoon to the storm, where gusts tug hard at the line.

💡 Hints when you’re stuck

A hint shows where the next piece belongs. And you can always take the last piece down again.

☀️ Truly relaxing

No timer, no energy system, no account. Just hang up the laundry and watch it dry.

Free and fair

Wäscheleine is free and funded by ads. Optional videos give you hints and coins – and the “No Ads” purchase removes the full-screen ads between levels for good.
