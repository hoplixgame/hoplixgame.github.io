At a glance

  • Drillnaut needs no account. We run no servers of our own and receive no personal data from the app.
  • Your progress is stored on your iPhone and – unless you turn it off – also in your own iCloud account. We have no access to it.
  • Drillnaut is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes data from your device – personalised ads only with your consent.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see payment details.

This policy covers the iPhone app “Drillnaut” and this website. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

2. Data on your device

The app stores your progress locally on your iPhone: drills, coins, gems, world progress, upgrades, challenges, purchased content and settings. This data is not sent to us.

3. Save backup in iCloud

If you are signed in to iCloud on your iPhone, the app keeps a copy of your progress in the iCloud storage of your Apple Account (Apple’s key-value store). This keeps your progress when you reinstall the app or use a new iPhone. The data is stored by Apple and can only be reached through your Apple Account – we cannot see it.

You can turn the backup off at any time in the app under Settings → iCloud. The legal basis is providing the feature you use (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). Apple’s processing is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

4. Ads with Google AdMob

To keep Drillnaut free, the app shows ads: optional reward videos and occasional full-screen ads at natural breaks (for example after travelling to another world). Ads are limited to content rated up to “PG”. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

Which data?

When loading and showing ads, Google processes in particular: IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information (e.g. device model, iOS version, language), information about ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic data. Your iPhone’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you allowed app tracking (see section 6).

Why?

To deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and – only with your consent – tailor ads to your interests.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent the app only shows limited, non-personalised ads where legally permitted.

Transfers to the USA

Google may transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework); standard contractual clauses also apply.

More: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

5. Managing your consent

Where required, the app asks you after the introduction, via a consent form from Google (User Messaging Platform), whether you agree to personalised ads and to storing or accessing information on your device. Your choice is stored on your device.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Ad privacy choices. Withdrawal applies to the future.

6. App tracking (Apple)

iOS asks whether Drillnaut may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. Only if you choose “Allow” does Google receive your device’s advertising identifier. You can change this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

7. Game Center

If you are signed in to Apple Game Center, the app sends your reached depth to the leaderboard and your achievements to Game Center. This is handled by Apple; your Game Center name may be visible to other players on the leaderboard. We receive no data about you. You can manage Game Center in iOS under Settings → Game Center.

8. In-app purchases

Purchases (e.g. “No Ads”, packs, gems) are handled exclusively by Apple. We receive no payment details and no data about your Apple ID. The app only learns from Apple which product was bought (with a transaction number) so it can deliver the content. This information stays on your device.

Apple’s processing is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

9. App Store and crash reports

Downloads and updates are handled by Apple. If you agreed in iOS to “share with app developers”, Apple provides us with anonymised statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you from them and only use them to fix bugs.

10. Children

Drillnaut is not specifically aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent with questions, please write to us.

11. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from attacks (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and loads no content from other servers.

12. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time. As we do not store data about you ourselves, most requests concern Google or Apple – but we are happy to help.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.

13. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
