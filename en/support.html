Frequently asked questions

How do I control Hoplix?

Tilt your iPhone left or right – or press and hold the left or right half of the screen. You can turn off tilt controls in Settings.

How do I unlock new worlds?

With stars: every level you finish gives you one star, and each level hides two bonus stars. The world map shows how many stars each world needs.

How do I use boosts?

Rockets, shields, magnets and coin boosters you own appear as buttons on the right side during a game – just tap them. Extra lives are offered on the “Ouch!” screen after a fall.

My purchase didn’t arrive.

Close Hoplix completely and start it again – open purchases are credited automatically at launch. You can get “Remove Ads” back under Settings → Restore Purchases, for example on a new iPhone.

If that doesn’t help, write to us with the date of purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I turn off the ads?

Yes – buy “Remove Ads” in the shop and the full-screen ads are gone for good. You can still watch optional videos for rewards if you want to.

How do I change my privacy and ad settings?

In the app under Settings → Privacy Settings (shown where required by law). You can change app tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in our privacy policy.

The leaderboard doesn’t open.

The leaderboard uses Apple Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone’s Settings, then tap “Leaderboard” in Hoplix again.

What are daily missions and events?

Every day there are three new missions with coin rewards – collect them in the menu under “Missions”. At Halloween and Christmas there are limited-time event worlds with an exclusive character as a reward.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, your progress is lost. When you move to a new iPhone using an iCloud or computer backup, it is transferred too.

I can’t hear any sound effects.

Check your iPhone’s silent switch or Action button and the volume. You can turn sound effects and vibration on and off in Settings.
