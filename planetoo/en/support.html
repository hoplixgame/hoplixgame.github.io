FAQ

How do I play Planetoo?

Drag to aim around the orbit and release to launch the planet. When two identical planets touch, they merge into a bigger one – all the way up to Sunny the sun. Two suns trigger a Supernova.

Keep every planet inside the dashed ring. If one stays outside for too long, your orbit overflows.

What do the boosters do?

The Black Hole swallows any planet you tap. Swap changes the planet on your launcher. You get new boosters for Stardust, in the Starter Pack or by watching an optional video.

What are frozen planets, comets, prisms and swirls?

From level 21 on, a new space phenomenon joins every ten levels: frozen planets thaw when two planets merge right next to them. Comets fly through the orbit – hit one to earn two extra launches. The prism joker makes the first planet it touches one size bigger. In a space swirl the whole orbit spins, so aim a little ahead.

You can read up on all of them in the Planetarium under “Space phenomena”.

What is the Daily Orbit?

There is a new challenge every day. Play every day to grow your streak. If you allow notifications, Planetoo reminds you in the evening. You can turn this off in your iPhone’s Settings under Notifications → Planetoo.

My purchase didn’t arrive.

Close Planetoo completely and start it again – pending purchases are credited automatically on launch. You can bring back “Remove Ads”, the Starter Pack and the Cosmic Pass under Settings → Restore purchases, for example on a new iPhone.

If that doesn’t help, write to us with the date of purchase. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I turn off the ads?

Yes – “Remove Ads” in the shop removes full-screen ads for good and adds 300 Stardust. After any other purchase, Planetoo also stops showing full-screen ads. You can still use optional reward videos whenever you like.

How do I change my privacy and ad settings?

In the app under Settings → Privacy options (shown where consent is required, e.g. in the EU and Switzerland). You can change app tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. See the Privacy Policy for details.

The ranking won’t open.

The ranking uses Apple Game Center. Sign in to Game Center in your iPhone’s Settings, then tap “Ranking” in Planetoo again.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. If you delete the app, your progress is lost. When you move to a new iPhone using an iCloud or computer backup, it comes along.

I can’t hear any sound.

Check the ring/silent switch or Action button and the volume of your iPhone. In Planetoo you can turn sound effects, music and haptics on and off under Settings.
