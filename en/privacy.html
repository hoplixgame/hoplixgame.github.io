Privacy Policy

for the app “Hoplix”

At a glance

  • Hoplix needs no account. We don’t run any servers of our own and receive no personal data from the app.
  • Your game progress stays on your iPhone.
  • Hoplix is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes data from your device for this – personalized ads only with your consent.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.
  • Leaderboards use Apple Game Center – only if you are signed in there.

This policy applies to the iPhone app “Hoplix” and to this website. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP). If there is any difference, the German version prevails.

2. Data on your device

The app stores your game progress locally on your iPhone: coins, stars, unlocked worlds and characters, purchased boosts, achievements and settings. This data is not sent to us. If you delete the app, your progress is deleted as well.

For tilt controls, the app reads your iPhone’s motion sensor while you play. These values are only used for steering and are neither stored nor transmitted.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

To keep Hoplix free, the app shows ads (full-screen ads and optional reward videos). The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

What data?

When loading and showing ads, Google processes in particular: IP address (and from it an approximate location), device and app information (e.g. device model, iOS version, language), information about ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic data. Your iPhone’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you have allowed app tracking (see section 5).

What for?

To deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and – only with your consent – tailor ads to your interests.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR; for accessing information on your device additionally Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG). Without consent, the app only shows limited, non-personalized ads where this is legally permitted.

Transfers to the USA

Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework); standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Managing your consent

Where required, the app asks you on first launch, using a consent dialog from Google (User Messaging Platform), whether you agree to personalized ads and to storing or accessing information on your device. Your choice is saved on your device.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Settings. Withdrawal applies to the future.

5. App tracking (Apple)

iOS asks whether Hoplix may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. Only if you choose “Allow” does Google receive your device’s advertising identifier. You can change this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases (boost packs, coin packs, “Remove Ads”) are processed exclusively by Apple. We receive no payment details and no data about your Apple Account. Apple only tells the app which product was bought (with a transaction number) so it can deliver the content. This information stays on your device.

Apple’s processing is covered by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. Game Center (leaderboards)

If you are signed in to Game Center on your iPhone, the app sends your best results (highest endless jump and number of stars) to Apple. They appear with your Game Center name on the leaderboards and are visible to other players. This processing is done by Apple; we only see the leaderboard entries.

You can sign out of Game Center or manage your profile at any time in your iPhone’s Settings under Game Center.

8. App Store and crash reports

Apple handles downloads and updates of the app. If you have agreed in iOS to “Share with App Developers”, Apple provides us with anonymized statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you from them and only use them to fix bugs.

9. Children

Hoplix is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent and have questions, please contact us.

10. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit it, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs in order to deliver the pages and protect them against attacks (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This website uses no cookies, no analytics tools and loads no content from other servers.

11. Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since we do not store any data about you ourselves, most requests concern Google or Apple – but we are happy to help.

You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority – in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.

12. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
