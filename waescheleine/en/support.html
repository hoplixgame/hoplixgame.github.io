FAQ

How do I play?

Drag the top piece from the basket in the bottom right up to the line. Once you’re close enough, your iPhone gives a short buzz – let go and the piece is pegged on. Let go too far away and it flies back into the basket.

Every piece pulls the line down. If nothing hangs into the paddling pool, the roses or the grass at the end, everything dries and the level is done.

How do I get three stars?

Stars depend on how far the line sags at the end. The coloured area in the middle under the line shows it: green for three stars, yellow for two, red for one. Heavy pieces like jeans, towels and sheets hang best close to the posts.

What happens with wind and storms?

In windy levels, gusts regularly pull the line a bit lower. A piece hanging just above the water can still dip in. If a piece turns red while you drag it, it would be at risk there during a gust.

What are coins for?

You earn coins for stars and for matching colours side by side. A hint (light bulb) shows where the next piece belongs. Undo takes the last piece down again. If you don’t have enough coins, both also work with an optional video.

My purchase didn’t arrive.

Close Wäscheleine completely and restart it – pending purchases are delivered automatically at launch. You can get “No Ads” back in the shop via Restore purchases, e.g. on a new iPhone.

If that doesn’t help, write to us with the purchase date. Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Can I turn off the ads?

Yes – “No Ads” in the shop removes the full-screen ads between levels for good. You can still use optional videos for hints and coins if you like.

How do I change my privacy and ad settings?

In the app via the privacy settings at the bottom of the level overview (shown where consent is required, e.g. in the EU and Switzerland). You change app tracking in iOS under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Details are in the privacy policy.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes your progress. When moving to a new iPhone via an iCloud or computer backup, it is transferred too.

I can’t hear any sound.

Check your iPhone’s silent switch or Action button and the volume. In the level overview you can switch sound on and off at the bottom.
