The game

Buy drills, drop two of the same kind onto each other and get a stronger one. Your big drill chews through block after block – layer by layer, planet by planet. And while you are away, your mine keeps on digging.

  • ⛏️ 40 drill levels to merge
  • 🪐 6 planets: Earth, Moon, Mars, Ice, Volcano & Alien
  • 💤 Keeps digging while the app is closed
  • 🎁 Treasure chests & supply balloons
  • 🔥 Overdrive for super-fast digging
  • ⚙️ Upgrades & machines for every world
  • ♻️ Prestige with relic cores and ancient tech
  • 📋 6 new challenges every day
  • 🏆 Over 30 achievements & leaderboard
  • ☁️ Save backup in iCloud

Free and fair

Drillnaut is free and funded by ads. No account, no waiting. Optional videos give you rewards like triple treasure chests or boosts – and with “No Ads” you get every reward instantly, without any video.
