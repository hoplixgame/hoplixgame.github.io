Privacy Policy

for the app “Wäscheleine”

At a glance

  • Wäscheleine needs no account. We don’t run any servers of our own and receive no personal data from the app.
  • Your game progress stays on your iPhone.
  • Wäscheleine is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Google processes data from your device for this – personalised ads only with your consent.
  • Purchases are handled entirely by Apple. We never see payment details.

This policy applies to the iPhone app “Wäscheleine” and to this website. It follows the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

2. Data on your device

The app stores your game progress locally on your iPhone: completed levels and stars, your coins, whether you bought “No Ads”, and the sound setting. This data is not sent to us. Deleting the app also deletes your progress.

3. Advertising with Google AdMob

To keep Wäscheleine free, the app shows ads: full-screen ads between levels and optional rewarded videos (e.g. for a hint, to take a piece down or for coins). Ads are limited to family-friendly content. The provider is Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland (“Google”).

What data?

When loading and showing ads, Google processes in particular: IP address (and an approximate location derived from it), device and app information (e.g. device model, iOS version, language), information about ads shown and tapped, and diagnostic data. Your iPhone’s advertising identifier (IDFA) is only used if you have allowed app tracking (see section 5).

What for?

To deliver ads, measure their performance, prevent fraud and – only with your consent – tailor ads to your interests.

Legal basis

Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Without consent the app only shows limited, non-personalised ads where legally permitted.

Transfers to the USA

Google may also transfer data to Google LLC in the USA. Google LLC is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework (and the Swiss-US Data Privacy Framework); standard contractual clauses apply in addition.

More: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

4. Managing your consent

Where required, the app asks you after the first levels, via a consent dialog from Google (User Messaging Platform), whether you agree to personalised ads and to storing or reading information on your device. Your choice is stored on your device.

You can change or withdraw your consent at any time in the app via the privacy settings at the bottom of the level overview. Withdrawal applies to the future.

5. App tracking (Apple)

iOS asks whether Wäscheleine may track your activity across other companies’ apps and websites. Only if you choose “Allow” does Google receive your device’s advertising identifier. You can change this at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

6. In-app purchases

Purchases (coin bag, coin basket, “No Ads”) are processed exclusively by Apple. We receive no payment details and no data about your Apple Account. The app only learns from Apple which product was bought (with a transaction number) so it can deliver it. This information stays on your device.

Apple’s processing is governed by the Apple Privacy Policy.

7. App Store and crash reports

Apple handles downloads and updates of the app. If you have agreed in iOS to “share analytics with app developers”, Apple provides us with anonymised statistics and crash reports. We cannot identify you from them and use them only to fix bugs.

8. Children

Wäscheleine is suitable for all ages but is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you are a parent with questions, please write to us.

9. This website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street, San Francisco, CA 94107, USA). When you visit, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver the site and protect it from attacks (legitimate interest, Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

The website uses no cookies, no analytics and loads no content from other servers.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, as well as the right to withdraw consent at any time. Since we store no data about you ourselves, most requests concern Google or Apple – but we are happy to help.

You may also lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Switzerland the Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC), in the EU the authority of your country of residence.

11. Changes

If the app or the law changes, we will update this policy. The version published here applies.
